Common Questions

Hoylake Members Ask…

Where exactly is Nomad Gym in Hoylake?

We're on Market Street, Hoylake — right in the village centre. Easy to find, easy to park nearby on the street or in the local car parks.

What makes Nomad different from other Hoylake gyms?

The infrared heated studio is genuinely unique on the Wirral. Add qualified coaching, real programming and a community with no ego — and you have something that doesn't exist elsewhere locally.

What membership options are available?

Multiple tiers from class-only through to unlimited full membership. All available month-to-month. Full details on the memberships page.

Do you cater for beginners?

All classes scale to the individual. Our coaches ensure newcomers are supported without holding back more experienced members — both groups train together effectively.

What are your Hoylake opening hours?

Mon–Thu 6am–8pm · Fri 6am–7pm · Sat 8am–1pm · Sun 8am–12pm. Early morning slots are the most popular among local commuters.

Can I try a class before joining?

Yes — reach out and we'll arrange a trial. No commitment, no hard sell. Just come and see what Nomad is about.
