Common Questions

Wirral Members Ask

Is Nomad Gym the only infrared studio on the Wirral?

Yes. As of 2025, Nomad is the only gym on the Wirral peninsula running far-infrared heated classes. If you want infrared fitness on the Wirral, this is it.

Where exactly is Nomad Gym?

We're on Market Street in Hoylake, CH47. There's free parking directly on Market Street and we're a short walk from Hoylake train station on the Merseyrail Wirral Line.

How long is the drive from other Wirral areas?

West Kirby is 5 minutes, Meols 3 minutes, Caldy 7 minutes, Heswall around 10 minutes via the A540. Most Wirral members are with us in under 15 minutes.

Do I need experience to join?

Not at all. Our classes are designed to be accessible at every fitness level. Instructors coach you through every session — there's no assumed knowledge and no intimidating floor.

What memberships are available?

We offer flexible monthly memberships with no long-term lock-in. Options include class passes and unlimited membership. Visit our memberships page for full details and current pricing.

Can I try a class before committing?

Absolutely. Get in touch via the contact form or email Jennifer@nomad-gyms.com to arrange a taster session. We'd rather you see what we're about before you sign anything.
