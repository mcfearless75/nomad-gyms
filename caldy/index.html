Common Questions

Caldy Members Ask…

How do I get to Nomad Gym from Caldy?

Head north on the B5192 towards West Kirby then take the A553 coast road to Hoylake. Market Street is approximately 7 minutes with no significant traffic.

Is parking straightforward?

Yes — Market Street has free on-street parking. Never had a Caldy member struggle to find a space.

Are the classes suitable for all levels?

All classes are designed to scale. Our coaches work with complete beginners and experienced athletes in the same session — you'll be pushed appropriately.

What membership tiers are available?

From single-class access through to full unlimited membership. All tiers available month-to-month with no long-term commitment required.

Do you offer a trial?

Yes — contact us and we'll get you in for a complimentary trial session. No sales pitch, no pressure.

What makes the infrared heat different?

Far-infrared wavelengths penetrate tissue rather than just heating the surrounding air. The result is a deeper metabolic and cardiovascular response at a lower perceived ambient temperature than conventional hot rooms.
