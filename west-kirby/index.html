Common Questions

West Kirby Members Ask…

How far is Nomad Gym from West Kirby?

Market Street, Hoylake is approximately 5 minutes by car from the centre of West Kirby via the A553. You can also cycle the coastal path in around 12 minutes.

Is there parking at Nomad Gym?

Yes — free on-street parking is available on and around Market Street in Hoylake, and there are public car parks within a short walk.

What classes are available for beginners?

All of our classes welcome beginners. Coaches scale every session to the individual — you'll never be left behind or made to feel out of place.

Do you offer a trial session?

Yes — drop us an enquiry and we'll get you in for a taster session. It's the best way to see if Nomad is the right fit before you commit.

What are your opening hours?

Mon–Thu 6am–8pm · Fri 6am–7pm · Sat 8am–1pm · Sun 8am–12pm. Early morning slots are popular with West Kirby commuters.

What makes Nomad different from other Wirral gyms?

The infrared heated studio, the quality of coaching, and the community. Nomad is not a box-ticking gym — it's a place people genuinely look forward to coming to.
